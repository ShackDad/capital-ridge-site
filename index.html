Building Smarter.
Building Better.

Construction expertise backed by over 12 years of real estate and development experience.

My name is Matt Shackell, and I have been involved in commercial real estate and development for over 12 years. Throughout my career, I have worked with countless clients who required reliable contractors for their projects. Time and time again, I found myself referring clients to construction companies — only to hear back that the pricing was far higher than expected, with limited transparency and little flexibility.

I understand that construction is not inexpensive. Rising material costs, increased labour expenses, and today's economic environment have all contributed to higher project costs. However, I also believe there is a smarter way to approach construction.

That is why I created Capital Ridge Construction.

Our advantage comes from our ability to operate efficiently while maintaining a highly skilled and dependable workforce capable of handling projects of any size. By keeping unnecessary overhead low and focusing on strong project management, we are able to deliver exceptional workmanship while providing our clients with competitive pricing.

Our goal is simple: complete every project on budget, on schedule, and to the highest standard possible.

At Capital Ridge Construction, we pride ourselves on being effective, knowledgeable, hardworking, and transparent. We work closely with our clients from start to finish to ensure every decision is made with value in mind. Our mission is to help you achieve the best possible results while maximizing your investment.

Whether it's a commercial build-out, renovation, addition, or custom project, Capital Ridge Construction brings the experience, relationships, and dedication needed to get the job done right.

Matt Shackell Founder, Capital Ridge Construction
